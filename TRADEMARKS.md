permission to use the Citrello name or logo.

## You're welcome to
- **Say what your work is or works with:** "a plugin for Citrello", "compatible with Citrello", "based on
  Citrello's code".
- **Write about Citrello** in articles, reviews, teaching material and talks.
- **Build and run unmodified copies** of the official releases under the name Citrello.

## Please don't
- **Release a modified version, or a fork, under the name Citrello** or a confusingly similar name. Forks are
  welcome; give yours its own name and logo, and say it's based on Citrello if you like.
- **Use the name or logo in a way that suggests** your product or service is made, endorsed or supported by
  Citrello when it isn't.
- **Register domain names, app-store listings, company names or social accounts** that use "Citrello" to
  represent anything other than the official project.
