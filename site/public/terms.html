Credits and Claude's checks

  • Claude's checks ("Check the claim", "What your sources say", "Find another reference") use credits: 3 credits for each cited paper Claude checks, and 8 credits to find another reference in your library. If a check fails, its credits aren't taken.
  • Every account gets 100 free credits once. They don't expire while the account exists.
  • Credits have no cash value, can't be transferred or exchanged, and are only for use in Citrello.
  • Claude's verdicts are help, not proof. Claude reads passages of the paper's text and can be wrong; check what matters before you rely on it. You are responsible for what you write and cite.
