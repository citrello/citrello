Citrello for ChatGPT and Claude

Give Claude or ChatGPT your own reference library, so they cite real papers you have, check them online, read their full text, and write Word or LaTeX documents with live citations. Free, and your library stays with you.

Before you start

  1. Citrello, with a free account. Use the desktop app (Windows or Mac) or Citrello in your browser, and sign in with your email under Settings. There's no password: Citrello emails you a code.
  2. Keep Citrello open while you use the connector. Your library lives on your computer or in your browser, not on our servers, so Claude and ChatGPT reach it through your open Citrello (the app running, or a citrello.app tab, which can stay in the background). It works from your phone too, while Citrello is open on a computer.

The connector's address is https://mcp.citrello.app/mcp

Connect

Claude

  1. Open Citrello in Claude's connector directory (or in Claude: Settings → Connectors → Browse connectors, search “Citrello”).
  2. Click Connect.
  3. Sign in with the same email as in Citrello and type the code we email you.
  4. In a chat, ask “What's in my Citrello library?”

ChatGPT

  1. In ChatGPT, open Settings → Apps (or Plugins) and find Citrello. Until it's listed there: turn on Developer mode under Advanced settings (paid plans) and create a connector with the URL above and OAuth.
  2. Sign in with the same email as in Citrello and type the code we email you.
  3. In a chat, ask “What's in my Citrello library?”

Claude Code

  1. In a terminal: claude mcp add --transport http citrello https://mcp.citrello.app/mcp
  2. In Claude Code, type /mcp, choose citrello and sign in.

What you can ask

  • “Find my papers on offshore wind decommissioning and cite two of them in this paragraph.”
  • “Add 10.1038/171737a0 to my library and check it's reliable.”
  • “Does Topham 2017 really say decommissioning costs are underestimated? Quote the passage.”
  • “Format the references in my ‘Thesis’ project in APA 7.” · “Find the style for Journal of Cleaner Production.”
  • “Write this section as a Word document with live citations in Vancouver style.”
  • “Give me BibTeX for my project” · “Check my LaTeX project's citations” · “Make an arXiv-ready zip.”

The tools

Your library
Search the library, show a reference, list projects, put references into a project.
Adding and checking
Add references from a DOI, a link or a typed reference (looked up on Crossref, duplicates avoided, rated verified / check / problem, e.g. retracted or a DOI that doesn't exist); verify references online; import RIS, BibTeX or CSL-JSON files, or your Zotero library from this computer.
Papers' full text
Read a paper's text and search inside your papers, for the papers whose text Citrello keeps (only the text, never the PDF). Add texts with the browser extension or by dropping PDFs into Citrello.
Citing and documents
Format references in 10,000+ citation styles; find a journal's style; check that every citation exists in the library; create a Word document with live citations and a bibliography, or update one.
LaTeX
BibTeX / biblatex entries; check a LaTeX project on your computer (missing, unused, unreliable references); make an arXiv-ready .zip. These work with files on your computer, so they need the desktop app. Tools that change things (adding, updating a Word file) ask before they run.

Privacy and help

Claude's or ChatGPT's requests pass through Citrello's server (mcp.citrello.app) to your open Citrello and the answer passes back; we don't keep their contents. Anthropic and OpenAI handle your chats under their own privacy policies. Details: privacy policy, terms.

“Citrello isn't open on the user's computer”
Open the Citrello app, or citrello.app/app in a browser tab, signed in with the same email you used to connect, and ask again.
Signed in with a different email?
Disconnect Citrello in Claude's or ChatGPT's settings and connect again with the right one.
To stop using it
Disconnect Citrello in Claude's or ChatGPT's settings. To delete your account, use Settings → Account in Citrello.

Questions or problems: hello@citrello.app.
