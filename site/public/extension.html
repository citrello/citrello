What it does

One click to add a paper
On any paper's page, click the Citrello button in your toolbar. Citrello finds the paper, checks it, and keeps its text when the page shows it.
Full texts for your whole library
In Citrello: Library → Import → Papers' full text → "Fetch the missing ones with my access". The extension opens each paper in a background tab with your login, takes its text and closes the tab. It goes slowly (one paper every 10–20 seconds), because publishers don't allow fast automatic downloading.
What it can see
It reads a page only when you click its button, or when it opens a paper you asked for. It asks for access to websites the first time you fetch full texts in bulk, not when you install it. Nothing goes to Citrello's servers: the text goes to your Citrello library.
