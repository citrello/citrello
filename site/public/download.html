Download Citrello

Free and open source. Your library stays on your computer. No account needed to start.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Download for Windows

Citrello-windows.zip: the app, the AI connector and the Claude extension.

Install

  1. Open the zip and drag the Citrello folder somewhere permanent, like Documents. Keep its three files together.
  2. Double-click Citrello.exe. Your browser opens Citrello.
Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because Citrello is new. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do this once.

Then, in Citrello's Settings

  1. Word: click Install Word button and restart Word. A Citrello tab appears.
  2. Claude: click Connect to Claude Desktop and drag the file it shows onto Claude's Extensions page.

Mac Beta

Apple silicon (M1 or newer)

Download for Mac

Citrello-mac.zip: the app, with the AI connector and the Claude extension inside.

Install

  1. Open the zip and drag Citrello into Applications.
  2. Double-click Citrello. macOS blocks it the first time: click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Citrello, and confirm with your password.
Citrello has no Dock icon. It runs in your browser at localhost:8585. Close it with Quit in its sidebar; open it again by starting Citrello.

Then, in Citrello's Settings

  1. Claude: click Connect to Claude Desktop and drag the file Finder shows onto Claude's Extensions page.
  2. Word: the Word tab is Windows-only for now. Use Documents → Update to format citations in Word files.

After installing

Add your references

Paste a DOI or a link into the search box and press Enter, import your Mendeley or Zotero library, or drag the Add to Citrello button to your bookmarks bar.

Connect your AI

Claude Desktop and Codex connect in one click. For ChatGPT or Claude on the web, sign in under Settings → Account and add the connector it shows.

Write

Ask your AI to write with your references, or insert citations from Word's Citrello tab. Change the journal style any time with Update.

Good to know

Internet
Citrello works offline with your library. It needs internet to look up new references, check them, and download citation styles.
Requirements for the extras
The Word tab needs Microsoft Word 2016 or newer (Microsoft 365 works). The Claude extension needs Claude Desktop. The ChatGPT connector needs a ChatGPT plan with Developer mode.
University computers
Citrello installs without administrator rights. If your university blocks unknown apps or Word macros, ask IT to allow Citrello: it's open source and keeps data on the computer.
Updates
New versions appear on this page. Download the new version and replace the old one; your library is kept.
Your data
Your library is in the Citrello folder in your user folder. To back it up or move it, copy that folder. Read the privacy policy.
Uninstall
Delete the app. On Windows, remove the Word tab first under Settings → Word → Remove. Delete the Citrello folder in your user folder only if you no longer want your library.
Older Intel Mac, or a problem?
Tell us on GitHub. Every version and its source code are on the releases page.
