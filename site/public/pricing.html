Pricing

Citrello is free: your library, citing in Word, 10,000+ styles, checking and fixing references, LaTeX and the AI connectors. Only Claude's citation checks cost something, because each one pays for Claude to read the paper. They use credits.

Free

For everyone

£0
  • Everything except Claude's checks
  • 100 credits to start (about 33 claim checks)
  • Free account, no card
Open Citrello

Student

For PhD and Master's students

£6 a month
  • 1,000 credits a month
  • About 330 claim checks a month
  • Find another reference in your library
Choose Student

Researcher

For researchers and supervisors who check more papers

£18 a month
  • 4,000 credits a month
  • About 1,330 claim checks a month
  • Find another reference in your library
Choose Researcher

Prices include VAT or sales tax where it applies. Payment on Stripe's page; your statement shows Link.com and Citrello. Cancel any time with "Manage plan" in Citrello.

Questions

Do credits carry over? A plan's credits renew every month and don't carry over. Your 100 free credits don't expire, and are used after the plan's.

Yearly plans? You pay once a year and still get the credits every month.

Running out? Citrello tells you before a check and doesn't take credits for checks that fail. Switch to Researcher any time with "Manage plan".

Where do I buy? In Citrello (web app, desktop app or Word): click your credits, then "Plans". See the terms.
