Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy covers Citrello, the reference manager, in all its forms: Citrello for desktop (the app for Windows and Mac), Citrello in the browser (citrello.app/app), Citrello for Word (the Word add-in), the Citrello browser extension, and the Citrello connectors for ChatGPT and Claude. It's made by Armin Hosseinghorbani (contact below).

Your library stays on your computer

References, projects, documents and the text of your papers are stored on your own computer: by the Citrello app in the Citrello folder in your user folder, or, when you use Citrello in the browser (citrello.app/app), inside your browser's own storage. Citrello for desktop never uploads them. Documents you check are read on your computer, not uploaded.

The cloud copy (web app, when you're signed in)

When you're signed in to Citrello in the browser (citrello.app/app), Citrello keeps a copy of your library (references, projects and the text of your papers, compressed) on its server, so it isn't lost if the browser's data is cleared and it comes back when you sign in on another computer. It's stored with Cloudflare in the European Union, only for your account, and replaced each time your library changes. You can turn it off in Settings → Library, which deletes it from the server; deleting your account deletes it too. Documents you check are never part of it.

"What your sources say" and "Check the claim": when you choose to ask Claude for verdicts, Citrello sends each cited sentence and a few short passages of the cited paper's text (not your whole document, and never the PDFs) to Claude, made by Anthropic, through Citrello's server, signed in with your account. Anthropic doesn't use them to train its models, and Citrello's server doesn't keep them: only your credit balance and a log of credits used (when, how many, and which kind of check). "Find another reference" looks only in your own library, in the same way: the sentence and passages of your papers' text.

Papers' full text

When you add a paper's PDF, or the browser button or extension finds the paper's text on its page, Citrello keeps only the text, next to the reference, on your computer (or in your browser). The PDF itself is not kept. ChatGPT or Claude read a paper's text only when you use them with Citrello and they ask for it, under their own privacy policies.

When Citrello goes online

  • To look up references: Crossref, doi.org, and the web page you add. If you enter your email in Settings, it is sent to Crossref with lookups, as Crossref asks.
  • To find free (open-access) copies of papers, if you ask for it: the papers' DOIs go to Europe PMC and Unpaywall. In the browser version, free PDFs are fetched through Citrello's server, because most repositories don't let web pages download them: only the DOI is sent, and the PDF passes straight to your browser without being stored.
  • To download citation styles from the Citation Style Language project on GitHub.
  • To reach your Mendeley or Zotero account, only if you connect them.
  • Once a day: an anonymous usage count (see below).
  • If you sign in: to keep a connection to Citrello's bridge (mcp.citrello.app), so ChatGPT and Claude on the web can reach your library.

Anonymous usage count

Once a day, while it runs, the app sends a random install ID made on your computer, the app version, your operating system and its version, whether it's the Microsoft Store version, and whether you're signed in. Nothing else: no name, email, library, documents or file names, and we don't record IP addresses with it. It tells us how many people use Citrello and which systems to support. Turn it off in the app under Settings → Account. Install IDs not seen for two years are deleted.

Account (optional)

If you sign in, we store your email address, when you signed up and were last active, the computers you signed in on (computer name, system and app version), and whether you agreed to emails about new features. We use this to let you use one account on several computers and connectors, to count how many people use Citrello, and, only if you agreed, to email you about new features. We keep it while your account exists, and delete it when you delete your account.

Connectors (ChatGPT, Claude on the web)

When a connector uses your library, its request passes through our server to your open Citrello (the app on your computer, or citrello.app in your browser), and the answer passes back. We don't keep the contents of these requests or answers. To keep you connected, we store the connection's sign-in tokens (only as one-way hashes), which connector they belong to (its name, for example "Claude" or "ChatGPT") and when they expire: one hour for the short-lived token, 180 days for the one that renews it. They're deleted within a day after they expire, when a connector tells us you disconnected, and when you delete your account. The connector's provider (for example OpenAI or Anthropic) handles what you send it under its own privacy policy.

Citrello for Word (the Word add-in)

Citrello for Word runs inside Word: its engine runs in the add-in's own storage on your computer, as Citrello in the browser does. It reads your document only to find, insert and update citations and the reference list, and to check the citations when you ask; the document's text is not sent to Citrello's servers. Citations are kept inside the document itself, with the papers' details, so the document works on any computer.

Without an account, it works with six sample papers, and nothing about you is stored. When you sign in (with your email and a code), it reads your library from your cloud copy (see above), and when it finds the free full text of a paper you cite, it adds that text to your library, so your cloud copy has it too. Checking references looks up their details (title, authors, DOI) on Crossref, doi.org and OpenAlex, and new citation styles are downloaded from the open CSL styles repository. "Check the claim" and "Find another reference" send Claude the cited sentence and short passages of the papers, as described above for "What your sources say"; to find a paper's free full text, its DOI is looked up on Unpaywall through Citrello's server (nothing is kept), and their results are remembered in the add-in's storage on your computer.

Browser extension

The Citrello extension reads a web page only when you click its button on that page, or when it opens a paper you asked it to fetch. It asks for access to websites the first time you fetch papers in bulk, not when you install it. What it reads (the paper's details and text) goes to Citrello on your computer or in your browser, never to Citrello's servers. It keeps a list of the papers you asked it to fetch until it has finished, and your choice of web or desktop Citrello.

Once you allow it to read websites, the extension can tell you when you open a paper that's in your library without its full text. For that it keeps, in your browser, a list of those papers (their DOI, title and Citrello key), and compares each page you open with it, on your computer. Nothing about the pages you visit is sent anywhere; the paper's text goes to Citrello only when you click "Add its text". You can turn this off in the extension's options.

Plans and payments

If you buy a plan, the payment is made on Stripe's page and sold to you by Link (Stripe's Managed Payments), which processes your payment details, tax and receipts under its own privacy policy. Citrello never sees your card. From Stripe, Citrello's server keeps your plan, its status and dates, and Stripe's customer and subscription IDs for your account, so your credits arrive and "Manage plan" works.

Emails

Sign-in codes are sent with Resend. Emails about new features are sent only if you agreed, and each one has an unsubscribe link. You can also turn them off in the app.

Your choices

Citrello works fully without an account. You can sign out of any computer, and delete your account and everything we store about you, in the app (Settings → Account).

Sharing

We don't sell or share your data. It is processed by Cloudflare (hosting), Resend (email), Anthropic (Claude's checks, only what you ask Claude to check) and, for plans, Stripe and Link (payments). There are no ads, no cookies for tracking, and no analytics on the website.
