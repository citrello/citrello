Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Your library stays on your computer

References, projects and documents are stored by the Citrello app on your own computer, in the Citrello folder in your user folder. They are never uploaded to or stored by Citrello's servers.

When Citrello goes online

  • To look up references: Crossref, doi.org, and the web page you add. If you enter your email in Settings, it is sent to Crossref with lookups, as Crossref asks.
  • To download citation styles from the Citation Style Language project on GitHub.
  • To reach your Mendeley or Zotero account, only if you connect them.
  • Once a day: an anonymous usage count (see below).
  • If you sign in: to keep a connection to Citrello's bridge (mcp.citrello.app), so ChatGPT and Claude on the web can reach your library.

Anonymous usage count

Once a day, while it runs, the app sends a random install ID made on your computer, the app version, your operating system and its version, whether it's the Microsoft Store version, and whether you're signed in. Nothing else: no name, email, library, documents or file names, and we don't record IP addresses with it. It tells us how many people use Citrello and which systems to support. Turn it off in the app under Settings → Account. Install IDs not seen for two years are deleted.

Account (optional)

If you sign in, we store your email address, when you signed up and were last active, the computers you signed in on (computer name, system and app version), and whether you agreed to emails about new features. We use this to let you use one account on several computers and connectors, to count how many people use Citrello, and, only if you agreed, to email you about new features.

Connectors (ChatGPT, Claude on the web)

When a connector uses your library, its request passes through our server to the Citrello app on your computer, and the answer passes back. We don't keep the contents of these requests. The connector's provider (for example OpenAI or Anthropic) handles what you send it under its own privacy policy.

Emails

Sign-in codes are sent with Resend. Emails about new features are sent only if you agreed, and each one has an unsubscribe link. You can also turn them off in the app.

Your choices

Citrello works fully without an account. You can sign out of any computer, and delete your account and everything we store about you, in the app (Settings → Account).

Sharing

We don't sell or share your data. It is processed by Cloudflare (hosting) and Resend (email). There are no ads, no cookies for tracking, and no analytics on the website.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/citrello/citrello, or email hello@citrello.app.
