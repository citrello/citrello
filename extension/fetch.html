Full texts with your access

The extension opens each paper in a background tab, with your university or library login, takes its text (the article on the page, or its PDF), closes the tab and gives the text to Citrello. The PDFs aren't kept.

It goes slowly on purpose (one paper every 10–20 seconds): publishers don't allow fast automatic downloading. Keep this tab and your Citrello tab open; you can use your browser meanwhile.
